ENTRY 01

Introduction

• The Problem is in most games nowadays is the environment feeling very static. Not a realistic world even with the high graphics players often have a lack of environment destruction. This lack of detail to the game creates a "ludonarrative dissonance" where the players actions don’t keep up with the environment's response. Leading to breaking immersion and keeping gameplay within a box.

• The Solution My project aims to fill in this gap left in most games by developing a fully destructible level area. Inspired by environmental mechanics such as Marvel Rivals and Battlefield. I have implemented systems where structures from walls to buildings react to damage done by the player.

• The Impact The player will end up gaining better gameplay feel with better player engagement by being immersed. As the world will be more interactive not limiting the player to go on a linear path. Giving the player a way to make it their own gameplay.

ENTRY 02

Aims

The aim of this project is to have a little level arena. That is fully destructible. That has a small aspect of gameplay which will be to collect objects around buildings by destroying them. Having fluid destruction and player engagement feeling realism.

ENTRY 03

Objectives

To achieve this aim I have done these. System understanding of how to make destructible objects in UE5. A clear level design so player can follow through the area. Implement well textured meshes to be destructible in the level. Iterative prototyping to get a game feel to make sure the feel of destruction feels right. Documenting a development process to later on reflect on all challenges and milestones.

ENTRY 04

Deliverables

• Final output of the project will have a fully playable level arena, that is a complete environment having buildings that are fully destructible. Fractured geometry assets of models in the level that break in real time. A collection system to pick up keys around the map to unlock the final door. VFX system on when destruction happens fire and smoke come out Technical reflective journal covering design implementation of the project.

ENTRY 05

Research Methodologies

• Industry best practice and literature review. To identify the best way to approach my project I conducted a thorough literature review of industry standard destruction techniques. My primary research being the GDC vault sessions especially the one “Smash the Scene” in Marvel rivals. As these sources provided a roadmap for balancing the way destruction works out without overloading the player.

• My technical workflow has been following YouTube tutorials. Following the specific pipeline gave me structure allowing to demonstrate Chaos Destruction from beginning to end. Following the steps of Fracturing the mesh and then Layering and using anchor fields to keep the building up to keep it structural , and physics field to create explosions and crumbling effects.

• I used iterative Prototyping to test out how a single bullet affected a simple cube and work my way up into meshes and multiple bullets to buildings collapsing. True success is measured by game feel making sure destruction feeling fluid and reactive rather than being a scripted event.

ENTRY 06

Research Methodologies

I draw a conclusion to whether my arena has the feel that makes the player feel more engaged for the feel of destruction with the core gameplay of collection keys.

So had to find special meshes that are textured on the inside. Most buildings are made as a brick. Which meant I had to switch to a dollhouse styled house.
