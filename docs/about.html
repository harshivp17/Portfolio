ABOUT ME

I am a Technical Games Designer specializing in Blueprint Logic and Level Design. Currently pursuing a B.Sc. in Computer Gameplay Design & Production at University of Staffordshire, I have over 5 years of experience in the forge of game development.

My focus lies in building immersive systems—from advanced AI Behavior Trees to complex Chaos Destruction. My project, Arman Station, was notably showcased for the BAFTA Games Industry Choice Award, reflecting my commitment to high-fidelity gameplay and technical excellence.
